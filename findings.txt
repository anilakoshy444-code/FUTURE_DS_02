# Customer Retention and Churn Analysis

## 1. Objective
To analyze customer churn, understand customer behavior, and identify ways to improve customer retention.

## 2. Key Findings
- Total customers analyzed: 7,032.
- Churned customers: 1,869.
- Retained customers: 5,163.
- Overall churn rate: 26.58%.
- Overall retention rate: 73.42%.
- Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate at 42.71%.
- Two-year contracts have the lowest churn rate at 2.85%.
- Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of 41.89%.
- Electronic check users have the highest payment-method churn rate at 45.29%.
- Churned customers have a shorter average tenure of 17.98 months, compared with 37.65 months for retained customers.
- Average monthly charges are higher among churned customers ($74.44) than retained customers ($61.31).

## 3. Recommendations
- Offer suitable discounts and benefits to encourage longer contracts.
- Contact month-to-month customers who may be considering leaving.
- Investigate customer concerns about fiber optic services and pricing.
- Review payment experiences for electronic check users.
- Provide better support and special offers to new customers.
- Monitor churn rates regularly to evaluate retention efforts.

## 4. Conclusion
The analysis shows that contract type, payment method, monthly charges, and customer tenure are associated with churn. Targeted offers and improved customer support may help businesses retain more customers.
